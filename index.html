Why copyright reform is necessary

Why my petition matters

Problem:

Everyday fans and creators experience videos getting blocked or taken down on platforms like YouTube, TikTok etc...

Rick Falkvinge's The Case for Copyright Reform
I suggest reading this book which will change your viewpoint even if you are the type of person who is skeptical. You can get the PDF for free on his website: https://falkvinge.net/books/

We propose:

  • Non-profit sharing becomes a fundamental human right worldwide.
  • No more blocks/restrictions or account loss.

Don't tolerate current laws. Be the person who takes action towards change.

Supporters:

YouTubers like Rick Beato and Charles Berthoud as well as activists like Cory Doctorow, Lawrence Lessig and Rick Falkvinge all talked about this issue.
